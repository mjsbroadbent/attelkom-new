How to connect

  1. Check the possibility of connection at your address on the coverage page or by calling 105.
  2. Come to a service centre with your passport and a document confirming your address (or the owner's permission).
  3. Sign the contract and pay for the connection.
  4. Our technician will visit you within 3–14 working days.

Archive tariffs «Internet 512», «Internet 1024» and «Family» are no longer available for new connections.
